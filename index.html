WAVE

Walk out, paddle through the surf, drop in when the DROP IN warning shows, and duck when it says DUCK.

Both hands go forward. One hand turns you, all the way around if you want to head back to the beach. The big button drops in, ducks or jumps. On the wave, the left hand climbs and the right hand drops away. Keyboard: left and right arrows together to go forward, space for the big button, down arrow to duck.

Tap or press a key to start
